    Heltec V3 and V4 SPI clock comparison, 23 October 2026

    Status: Completed. Raising SPI from 8 MHz to 8.888888 MHz saved less than 0.6% of average complete radio-profile switching time in these measurements. The production defaults remain 8 MHz on these two boards.

    Results

    Each result combines both switch directions and two measured windows per clock. Means are weighted by the actual number of complete switches.

    Board Change between profiles 8 MHz mean, us 8.889 MHz mean, us Saved, us Saved, % Samples, 8 / 8.889 MHz
    Heltec V3 Frequency only 774.608 774.146 0.462 0.060 3769 / 3743
    Heltec V3 Frequency and modem 909.121 904.614 4.507 0.496 3540 / 3801
    Heltec V4.3 OLED Frequency only 557.113 554.125 2.988 0.536 3700 / 3665
    Heltec V4.3 OLED Frequency and modem 685.295 682.891 2.404 0.351 3666 / 3667

    The V3 frequency-only difference is smaller than its variation between windows. All results are descriptive observations from two windows per clock, not statistical confidence estimates. These are instrumented means, not maximum switching times or production timing guarantees.

    Board / profile change Range of window means at 8 MHz, us Range at 8.889 MHz, us
    V3 / frequency only 773.505-775.697 773.251-775.052
    V3 / frequency and modem 907.838-910.219 903.381-905.868
    V4 / frequency only 556.939-557.287 552.863-555.367
    V4 / frequency and modem 685.169-685.419 682.745-683.038

    The faster clock produced small improvements in individual bus-related stages: the frequency stage improved by 0.54-1.18 us, and mixed-modem changes improved modulation by 0.98-1.28 us and receive restart by 1.41-1.68 us. Other stages varied in both directions. Stage getters have separate sample boundaries, so their means must not be added to reconstruct the whole-switch measurement or treated as proof of a single bottleneck.

    Physical setup and measurement

    Both boards ran their Full observer MQTT Repeater targets from source 601f3ee622621189b4acaa229639c4ef5be910f2. Matching private test builds retained the normal features and added these four flags:

    -DMC_SX1262_SPI_HZ=8888888
-DMESH_HIL_ESP_SPI_AB=1
-DMESH_HIL_PROFILE_TIMING=1
-DMESH_HIL_PROFILE_HISTOGRAM_COUNT=8

    The same installed image was used for both clocks on each board. Explicit radio.fast.spi=8 selected 8 MHz; radio.fast.spi=0 selected the test image's 8.888888 MHz default. All seven fast-switch feature settings, CPU selection, power-saving policy, WiFi, Bluetooth and logging settings remained unchanged. This comparison does not isolate differences between V3 and V4 hardware or their target configurations.

    Actual SPI divider readings, taken after a transaction began, confirmed both clocks on both boards before and after every measurement window:

    Clock, Hz Actual divider
    8000000 0x00101001
    8888888 0x00082001

    The two receive-profile pairs were:

    Pair / profile Frequency, MHz BW, kHz SF CR Preamble, symbols
    Both pairs / primary 917 62.5 7 5 32
    Frequency only / secondary 918 62.5 7 5 32
    Mixed modem / secondary 918 500 5 5 200

    For each pair, clocks alternated 8 / 8.889 / 8 / 8.889 MHz. Each window contained 30 seconds without CLI getters, after effective profile application and four successful self-test switches in each direction. The separate 120-second recommendation-display cooldown could remain pending; it does not prevent the scanner from switching or the timing capture from recording.

    Eleven instrumented stages covered the complete RX-to-RX path, including admission, standby, frequency and modem configuration, and restarting receive. The whole-switch mean was independently recomputed from atomic count and sum deltas. Reads at window boundaries add a short, recorded span around the central quiet period. No intentional RF test packets were transmitted; ambient reception and ordinary firmware traffic were not controlled.

    All 16 windows passed, totaling 29,551 complete-switch samples. There were no new core error flags, switch errors or RXPS-watchdog recoveries. Both boards already had core flag 8 and recovery history soft=1,hard=0 before the matrix, following their saved five-minute idle radio watchdog. Those baseline values were retained and checked, rather than reset or counted as a new SPI failure. Uptime advanced without a detected reboot during the measurements.

    The compact histogram's final bucket is open-ended at 448 us. Its tail does not establish exact percentiles. Cumulative minima and maxima also cannot be reported as per-window extrema. In particular, these average results do not establish a 500 us maximum or qualify packet delivery, CPU sleep or Bluetooth.

    The first controller attempt stopped before collecting a window because it checked asynchronously applied radio2 settings too early. A revised controller waited for the effective profiles. A subsequent preflight detected the idle watchdog baseline; it was diagnosed and explicitly recorded before the final successful matrix. Neither incomplete attempt contributes timing samples.

    Firmware restoration and evidence

    Temporary radio profiles and the original saved SPI selection were restored after measurement. Both boards were then restored to normal, uninstrumented Full observer MQTT Repeater images from source 601f3ee6, using the original 8 MHz default. Application-only installation and complete application readback passed; partition tables, OTA selection metadata and readable saved settings were preserved. A final read-only inventory confirmed the same source and identity, SPI selection 0 resolving to 8 MHz, all seven fast features on, radio2 off, temporary profiles cleared, no test diagnostics and core flags 0.

    Normal firmware Verified application body
    Heltec V3 736232F76F3C8667
    Heltec V4.3 OLED CF58F8FF6B71A565

    These restoration checks have separate receipts; they are not inferred from the timing measurement's settings cleanup.

    The sanitized results retain per-direction means, all eleven stages, counts, window-mean ranges, clock proofs and restoration checks. Private hardware identities and raw CLI responses are excluded.

    Validation included five native diagnostic tests, four timing-capture tests, two portable-HAL tests, 14 controller tests, 18 installer tests and nine independent analysis tests. All four canonical Full images passed their capacity checks without feature reductions. Hardware timing and restoration receipts provide the physical evidence; the native tests validate capture, control and analysis behavior.
