    Shipping Morse results

    The unchanged Full RAK4631/MeshPocket images were tested at 902.1 MHz with requested chip TX power at most -9 dBm, three different messages, 25/60 WPM, both directions and two repeats. No HIL threshold, timing or sampling override was enabled. All 24 decoded messages mismatched. Thirty-second no-transmitter controls produced 20 characters on RAK and four on Pocket.

    Wanted peaks across all 24 trials ranged from -50.5 to -46.0 dBm; the 25 WPM subset ranged from -50.5 to -47.5 dBm. Maximum observed valid sample gaps were 2-3 ms. Quiet RSSI excursions reached -110.5 dBm on RAK and -92.5 dBm on Pocket, above their roughly -126/-125 dBm floors. This supports an automatic-detection qualification failure, without identifying external RF interference versus receiver-generated excursions. The reported bad counter is consecutive read failures, not a cumulative guarantee of no SPI errors. All decoded capture lengths matched their firmware headers and reached the end marker, excluding host paging truncation in these trials. Source inspection shows automatic squelch can admit sustained quiet excursions and corrupt mark or gap boundaries; RSSI extrema alone do not establish those pulse durations.

    A separate five-minute Pocket quiet capture expired automatically after 300.40 seconds, retained seven false characters, and returned the same capture on repeated firmware paging. Lifecycle/retrieval passed; silence detection failed. Original settings were read back after both campaigns. Historical successful HIL fixed-threshold trials do not qualify the shipping detector.
