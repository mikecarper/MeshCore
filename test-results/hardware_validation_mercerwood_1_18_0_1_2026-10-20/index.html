    Mercerwood bench qualification: 12 active radios, 20 October 2026

    21 October follow-up: the user lifted MeshPocket's bootloader-work exclusion. Its fresh Full Companion USB, BLE, contact persistence/sync and three two-way LoRa rounds passed in the MeshPocket qualification. The exclusion and source-bound measurements below describe the earlier 20 October campaign; the other twelve boards were not retested for this update.

    Status: PASS. 12/12 active boards have complete accepted bench coverage; 0 have a latest required failure and 0 remain incomplete. The original inventory has thirteen radios: eleven Full Companions and two Full OTA repeaters. The active target is twelve radios: ten Companions and two repeaters. MeshPocket was operator-excluded at this campaign's close for separate bootloader work.

    All thirteen 0865 candidate builds passed. Companion updates target 08650f644b680a5dfaacb95142e3cef8529da9d2; the table records each board's actual installed application. Both repeaters retain actual c46defaefd642973cbbdb473815d56b5b261cb2e (C46). Test-only commit 190064f4b5fa97e75dcfca7876568b757ba43b31 passed all twelve CI jobs. Build manifest SHA-256: 1dbf13ae8a3419f2c8e12d0432c37fa990d5bcfbb5649ab4d1d4ed4cec21a468. Seven active nRF52 boards retain the source 88d39510140ce6894cf8e74b93cbd28d7387fa21 bootloader baseline; Companion application-only updates transferred no bootloader or SoftDevice data.

    Companion USB checks cover 100 query cycles, ten reopens, owned CRUD, persistence through warm restart and applicable radio2 checks. BLE covers three authenticated thirty-second sessions, exact USB-bound identity and device information, clean core state and strict controller lifecycle. Two-way RF verifies exact zero-hop DEV datagrams, counters and owned channel/radio-lease cleanup. Each contact trial verifies 64 owned full-stream records and a 32-record timestamp subset, then removes them and proves absence after reopen while preserving unrelated records. Repeater coverage uses ten query cycles, three reopens, verified warm restart and three nonce-bound RF rounds. Gateway retains its earlier C46 query/reopen baseline; its new held warm restart and RF rounds keep separate intervals and evidence digests. SD coverage is read-only metadata, not media integrity.

    T1000-E's eleven physical USB-priority phases passed. These qualify the USB-priority policy, not simultaneous active USB/BLE. The first current cold-host-release strict failure remains recorded before one later explicit unchanged-firmware retest passed. XIAO nRF52's original unaligned QSPI failure was corrected by shared buffer-alignment fix 714cfc4546ac43c220d06abff9adb128f58d16dd; its earlier strict BLE 0x3E failure also remains recorded before the later clean same-bond result.

    T-Deck and XIAO ESP32-S3 later passed strict three-session BLE with USB connected and host WiFi quiet; earlier extra-0x3E failures remain in the history. WiFi was checked at trial boundaries, not continuously. T-Deck's initial two WiFi-on failures predate its current boot, so the observations do not isolate WiFi from boot/current-PIN/state differences. Its diagnostic wrapper retained a restoration-check FAIL; later read-only records separately verified handback. XIAO's original wrapper passed handback. Separate exact 64/32 contact trials, secure controller proofs and cleanups later passed with normal WiFi restored. WiFi association at 2437 MHz was observed before and after those trials. No WiFi root cause or firmware fix is established.

    V4's earlier contact setup refusal preceded a later bounded-transition trial and exact cleanup. Tower's CAD2 history and Pocket's pre-DFU core8 refusal remain recorded before their separate warm preparations; preparations are not qualification passes. Tower's cumulative receive/read-error count does not prove CRC failure. Gateway's earlier warm failure had 43 complete responsive snapshots with advancing uptime, not a proven hang. Its later CAD2 baseline refusal came after the service was stopped. A separate one-request warm preparation preceded the qualification's own warm restart and RF3; the exact service/configuration owner then returned. Latest accepted results do not relabel those failed attempts.

    These finite checks do not establish RF range, phone interoperability, long-duration reliability or every firmware feature. Earlier source-bound results remain in the fleet follow-up, diagnosis follow-up, and initial USB-priority report.
