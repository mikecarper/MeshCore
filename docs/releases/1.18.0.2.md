# MeshCore 1.18.0.2 - USA Cascade

**In preparation.** The final release has not been published. These notes
describe changes from 1.18.0.1; board and role support varies.

[Firmware picker](../firmware_picker.md) | [Update guide](../ota_user_guide.md) |
[Features beyond mainline in 1.18.0.1](1.18.0.1.md#extra-features)

## New and improved

- **Easier LoRa firmware updates:** the [browser updater](../browser_lora_ota.md)
  can prepare compatible radios and select an update channel automatically.
  Older firmware may need a compatibility mode.
- **Optional rotary controls:** supported devices can use a rotary encoder,
  with configurable pins for supported external controls and indicators.
- **Seven-day high and low readings:** `get telemetry.minmax` reports
  temperature and battery-voltage history around each day's extremes.
- **More nRF52 bootloader options:** selected Repeater and Room images include
  the matching OTAFIX bootloader. Additional board-specific images use an
  untested preview bootloader; see [board details](../nrf52_bootloader_previews.md).
- **Radio2 switching:** supported ESP32 boards use faster radio switching;
  actual timing and reception depend on the board and settings.

## Fixed

- **W12 radio changes:** configured radio settings apply after the reply delay
  instead of requiring a reboot. W12 also gains frequency calibration controls.
- **WiFi setting:** turning WiFi off persists across reboot. The web interface
  has its own separate setting.
- **SX1262 reception:** AGC protection is enabled on supported SX1262 boards
  and retained across radio sleep.
- **33 dBm radio modules:** board-specific drive limits and variants address
  incorrect power settings on supported external-amplifier boards.

## Choosing and installing firmware

The final downloads are not published yet. Until then, the
[nRF52 bootloader preview](https://github.com/mikecarper/MeshCore/releases/tag/nrf52-bootloader-preview-v1.18.0.2-9d961a19)
provides the additional board-specific firmware variants.

Choose the exact board and role. On ESP32, an app-only update requires a
compatible partition layout. On nRF52, ordinary images keep the installed
bootloader; images marked **with bootloader** carry a matching update.
Recognized compatible bootloaders are checked after startup. See the
[update guide](../ota_user_guide.md) and [bootloader guide](../nrf52_embedded_bootloader.md).

Fresh USA Cascade defaults are **910.525 MHz / BW62.5 / SF7 / CR5**. Saved
radio settings take precedence.

## Testing and limits

The consolidated firmware passed build, memory, package and checksum checks.
Hardware coverage varies; the seven additional nRF52 board families in the
bootloader preview have not been physically tested by this project.

- ThinkNode M4, ThinkNode M8 and Wio WM1110 Dev Kit do not have a bundled
  OTAFIX image in this release.
- Muzi Base stock bootloaders are not automatically replaced; the
  [preview instructions](../nrf52_bootloader_previews.md) describe the local route.
- Radio2 profiles share a radio; simultaneous reception is not guaranteed.

Most firmware uses source [a723af94](https://github.com/mikecarper/MeshCore/commit/a723af94ed7d8036329fb1f57637e6a009864752).
The additional nRF52 bootloader-preview images use
[9d961a19](https://github.com/mikecarper/MeshCore/commit/9d961a199325ca9f7d059e8c103f0a80e4698ef1).
