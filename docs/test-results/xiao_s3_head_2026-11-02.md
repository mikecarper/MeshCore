# XIAO ESP32-S3 WIO HEAD validation — 2 November 2026 UTC
<p class="meshcore-hosted-doc-link"><a href="https://mikecarper.github.io/MeshCore/test-results/xiao_s3_head_2026-11-02/">View this page on MeshCore Docs</a>.</p>

**Application and USB pass; conditional single-profile LoRa pass.** The
Mercerwood XIAO ESP32-S3 WIO runs
`v1.18.0.2-halo-keymind-cascade-dev-observer-8cc67955 (Build: 02-Nov-2026)`
from [8cc67955a](https://github.com/mikecarper/MeshCore/commit/8cc67955a127fa3de7d5c780254e31a5cb35067b).
The role remains Repeater. Discovery delivery with a scanning peer remains
unqualified: earlier and final misses are retained below.

The exact USB identity, physical path, application public identity and reported
`Xiao S3 WIO` model selected the `Xiao_S3_WIO_repeater_observer_mqtt` Full
target. A complete private 8 MiB flash backup preceded installation. The
2,106,712-byte application fits the existing 3,342,336-byte active slot at
`0x10000`; its SHA-256 is
`eb72ef8670a5e0b09cfc2bf8d9ea78f26de2c11924fe12d061063754c81990e5`.
Application readback matched, partition table and OTA metadata were unchanged,
and the installer wrote no other flash ranges. No bootloader, merged image or
partition migration was installed. Running `ota self` matched body fingerprint
`ab310e27e16e0421`.

Initial USB checks passed twelve board/version/core cycles and three reopens.
A commanded warm reboot preserved the identity and checked saved preferences,
with uptime falling from 404 to 10 seconds and zero core flags or queued packets.
The subsequent stress check passed **100 board/version/core cycles and ten
fresh reopens**, including 354 commands with exact echo and complete reply
association. Uptime remained continuous, core flags and queues stayed zero,
and maximum measured command latency was 0.125419 seconds. Final restoration
and the individual samples are recorded in the
[sanitized evidence](xiao_s3_head_2026-11-02.safe.json).

The linked memory admission check passed: 240,456 internal bytes available
against 230,288 required. Runtime reported approximately 8 MiB PSRAM.
These are startup and idle observations; they do not qualify maximum MQTT,
TLS/WSS or browser load.

The LoRa peer was the freshly identity-verified T096 Full Companion on the same
HEAD. Its primary stayed at 904.525 MHz, 62.5 kHz, SF7, CR 4/5 and TX −5 dBm.
The XIAO used a two-minute RAM-only primary lease matching that PHY, with an
explicit 128-symbol preamble. Its discovery responses and saved local advert
eligibility were enabled for each test, then restored. RX power saving was off.
Each of three requests per trial had a fresh nonce and a twenty-second receive
window. Success required the complete XIAO public key, matching nonce and
zero-hop discovery response; local command acceptance alone did not count.

| Trial | T096 receiver | XIAO TX setting | Identity-bound replies | XIAO RX / TX delta | New receive errors |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original | Primary plus RX secondary | 2 dBm | **1/3** | 3 / 6 | 0 on both |
| Lower-power comparison, after warm reboot | Primary plus RX secondary | −9 dBm | **0/3** | 3 / 6 | 0 on both |
| Single-profile comparison | Primary only | 2 dBm | **3/3** | 3 / 6 | 0 on both |
| Return to original receiver configuration, same XIAO boot | Primary plus RX secondary | 2 dBm | **2/3** | 3 / 6 | 0 on both |

The saved RX secondary was 902.750 MHz, 250 kHz, SF7, CR 4/5, explicit preamble
96. It was disabled only for the single-profile comparison, with the exact
manual configuration restored afterward. No T096 primary temporary lease,
offline-message read or queue-draining OTA allocation was used. Rate-limit
windows were allowed to clear before additional trials. The return trial also
waited until the restored scanner's timing self-test reported ready.

The three single-profile replies arrived in 3.6652, 1.9331 and 4.5982 seconds.
Received reply RSSI was −5 dBm, indicating a strong bench link. Lowering XIAO
power did not establish a remedy. Additional XIAO transmissions include the
automatic advertisements started by TempRadio; packet TX counts are not
discovery-response counts. The small captured OTA advertisements were
distinguished from full discovery replies.

This comparison associates the misses with the tested scanning configuration,
but does not identify a scanner defect, missing transmission or RF root cause.
There was no independent passive witness for the missed replies. XIAO core
flags remained zero. T096 already had sticky watchdog bit 8 before these RF
tests, with `soft=1,hard=0`; both the bit and recovery counts stayed unchanged.
That history is consistent with its thirty-minute receive-inactivity recovery,
without establishing the original cause. Neither device recorded new receive
errors, and the T096 scanner recorded zero switch failures.

The first post-flash query with a 0.3-second settle failed the board-readiness
check. Its rejected raw reply was not captured, so its cause remains unassigned.
One-second-settled checks subsequently passed. A separate lower-power setup
stopped before sending RF because it requested a test-build-only TX-status
getter; all settings were restored before the measured comparison. These
preparation observations are retained and are not counted as RF rounds.

The original XIAO identity and checked settings were restored: radio direction
off, both advert intervals zero, primary 902.75/250/7/5, TX 2 dBm, manual
secondary off and no temporary profiles. T096 identity, device preferences,
contacts, channel names/keys and checked saved policies were preserved. Both
serial endpoints were released. Battery GPIO readings were treated as unknown;
calibration, charging configuration and emergency-power policy were unchanged.

This validation introduced no source changes and establishes no historical fix.
Battery cold boot, WiFi joining, sustained forwarding/range, LoRa OTA
installation, bootloader updates and loaded observer operation remain outside
this check. The [October 28 campaign](hardware_validation_mercerwood_1_18_0_2_2026-10-28.md)
remains historical evidence; its unresponsive programmer result is not rewritten.
