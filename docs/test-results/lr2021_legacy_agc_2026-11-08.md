# LR2021 legacy calibration on W12 - 2026-11-08

The final `feb7c43dd` candidate passed independent legacy calibration, guarded
primary restoration, multi-SF RF checks and a real 240-second combined primary
plus old-calibration cycle on the Meshnology W12, with no reported failure or
unplanned recovery. Supporting `0d880be86` runs also passed native RXPS and single-profile
multi-SF. The normal build passed its final 3/3 RF check, and both radios'
recorded settings were restored and verified.
These are functional bench results, not sensitivity or lossless-traffic proof.

## Setup and source

A Meshnology W12 ran diagnostic Full Companion firmware with
`MESH_AGC_TIMING_DIAGNOSTICS=1`. A RAK Tag sent unique direct `RAW_CUSTOM` probes.
These were nearby-radio tests with matched tuples, not controlled weak-signal
or power measurements. Dual-profile probes used 909.5/919.5 MHz, SF7 and
128-symbol transmitted preambles; multi-SF probes varied the primary SF.

- Final hardware candidate: `feb7c43dd`; regression coverage: `e61c08960`.
- Supporting full-mode hardware results: `0d880be86`.
- Supporting independent legacy tests used `set agc 0`, `set agc.old 4`.
- Counts below cover individual test windows, not the whole boot.

After the combined cycle, PRAM was on and hooked, with zero uncertainty,
recoveries, bad handoffs, lost preambles, device/command/sequence errors and
pending repair. All four native old-helper calls took 26.301-26.357 ms;
admission to continuous RX took 33.787-33.864 ms.

The combined check waited for the actual 240-second cadence. Its roughly
250-second observation included nine-second polling and RF probes; that is
not the duration of a maintenance operation or an RF outage. The cold restore
belongs to primary restoration: the separate old-helper increment proves it
was not silently counted as satisfying the automatic old half.

## Implementation principles

Legacy maintenance uses checked RC-standby `Calibrate(0x6F)` and frontend
calibration at the actual current frequency and all enabled profile frequencies.
Correction is applied once; encoded 4 MHz points and RF paths are deduplicated
into up to three frontend slots. Missing frontend coverage (`0x0200`) is
repairable by that sequence; unrelated busy-trigger and oscillator/PLL-lock
faults remain preserved and rejected.

Ordinary single-profile/RXPS legacy calibration includes retained sleep/wake.
A known-resident PRAM/LDO session omits only that sleep/wake step, retaining
full checked calibration and verified scanner resume.

Primary duty-cycle refresh does sleep. Its owned cold restoration preserves
packet/pulse and generation guards, performs an actual checked radio reset,
calibrates both enabled profiles, then reloads and verifies PRAM. It never
rewrites an active image. Descriptor words alone do not prove working code
or mapping; native version, `SCN3` identity, hook and generations are checked.

Cold restoration can add full calibration below the 240-second policy
threshold. It belongs to primary restoration and does not satisfy the separate
automatic old half at 240 seconds or longer. The final combined test verified
both stages. Pure queued noise-floor requests do not block the checked matching
paused-PRAM maintenance lease; active sampling batches/windows still do.

## Limits and validation status

Earlier concurrent-traffic stress encountered a post-stop activity IRQ
(`001100A0`) with FIFO level 0. The safety guard refused the handoff and bounded
recovery selected host fallback. That traffic case remains unresolved by the
calibration fixes; PRAM/multi-SF can remain off until profile change or reboot.

Timing covers software boundaries, not RF downtime or analog AGC state.
Native-helper samples include sleep/wake only on the single-profile path.
Admission-to-continuous-RX includes host restoration but excludes the earlier
scanner pause/standby and later rehook. No calibrated sensitivity, battery
saving or interference-performance claim follows from these tests.

All 165 focused host tests passed (68 LR2021 and 97 shared), as did the final
full PRAM suite and normal W12 build. The W12 application uses 2355688 of
6553600 bytes. LILYGO X1 Repeater and Companion builds passed; X1 has build-only
evidence.

## Normal build and final cleanup

The W12 was left running the normal `feb7c43dd` Full Companion image with timing
diagnostics disabled. With `agc=0` and `agc.old=4`, it received 3/3 additional
probes and reported `radio.apply code=0 repair=0`. The normal image SHA-256 is
`057ea2302e5518b09c6eb6a7c988a120181c09cac26349522a1e1bb13e89c977`.

Final reads verified both radios back on 910.525 MHz, BW62.5, SF7, CR4/5 with
automatic preambles. W12 transmit power is 5, trim is 9,9, primary multi-SF5-8
is on, radio2 is off with automatic operation allowed, and cross-repeat is off.
Power saving is on; desired RXPS remains level 6 with preamble 16 and its
expected continuous-RX hold for multi-SF. Maintenance is `agc=16`,
`agc.old=auto`. The RAK Tag has transmit power 22, `agc=16`, old maintenance off,
RXPS off, power saving off and radio2 manually off. Both serial ports were
released after verification.
